## What it is

A month-long online hackathon run by lablab.ai together with AssemblyAI, the Voice AI infrastructure company (its speech-to-text, voice agent, and speech-understanding models power products like Granola, HeyGen, Ashby, and ClickUp). Every participant builds on AssemblyAI. Open to all skill levels — tutorials go live before kickoff, and mentors are available in Discord throughout.

Tagline: **"The fastest path to a working voice agent."**

## The challenge

Build a voice AI agent using AssemblyAI's real-time voice AI technology. Two paths, pick based on how much of the voice stack you want to own:

### Path 1 — Voice Agent API (full stack, managed)
End-to-end voice agent through a single connection; AssemblyAI handles the core voice interaction stack.
- Speech-to-text powered by Universal-3 Pro
- LLM routing and voice output
- Turn-taking and voice activity detection
- JSON-Schema tool calling
- Designed for fast, natural voice interactions

### Path 2 — Realtime Speech-to-Text API (bring your own orchestration)
Use AssemblyAI's real-time STT as the foundation; bring your own LLM/TTS/orchestration.
- Real-time speech-to-text over WebSocket
- Sub-second transcription
- Multilingual speech recognition
- Bring your own LLM and text-to-speech
- More control over voice agent architecture

### Getting API access
Sign up for an AssemblyAI API account via the link on the hackathon page to claim free credits. **Must accept cookies during sign-up.** If you already have an account, log out first, then use the hackathon sign-up link again to log back in and activate hackathon credits.

## Prizes

**5 winners, $1,000 cash + $1,000 in API credits each** (no ranked 1st/2nd/3rd — five equal winner slots).

Notes from the page: participation is voluntary; prizes/opportunities depend on eligibility, availability, and third-party sponsors; rules/prizes/terms can change or be canceled at organizer discretion; submissions must be original and MIT-license-compliant; prize distribution can take up to 90 days.

## Judging criteria

1. **Application of Technology** — how effectively the chosen model(s) are integrated into the solution.
2. **Presentation** — clarity and effectiveness of the project presentation.
3. **Business Value** — impact and practical value, how well it fits business areas.
4. **Originality** — uniqueness/creativity of the solution and ability to demonstrate behaviors.

## What to submit

- **Basic info:** project title, short description, long description, technology & category tags

## How to participate

- Register on both the lablab.ai platform and the lablab.ai Discord server.
- Click "Enroll" at the bottom of the hackathon page; read the Hackathon Guidelines, Getting Started Guide, and lablab.ai Hackathon Rule Book.
- No prior AI/coding experience required.
- Before kickoff, browse lablab.ai's AI Tech and tutorials pages for background.
- **Teams:** 1–6 people. Team-finding happens on the dashboard or Discord.
- **Company email tip:** using a company email when registering/submitting is encouraged (not required) — helps AssemblyAI identify who's building with them and share follow-up opportunities. Personal emails are fine too.
